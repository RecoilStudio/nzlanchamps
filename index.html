FAQ

Early days, so a few answers are still "we'll tell you soon".

When exactly is it?

Waitangi weekend 2027, in early February. We will lock the exact days once the venue is booked.

Is registering interest the same as entering?

No. It tells us who is keen so we can plan. Team entries open later and will be announced to everyone on the list.

Will there be other games?

At this stage, this event is Counter-Strike 2 only.

Will there be prizes?

That is the plan. The size depends on entries and sponsors, and will be announced before entries open.

I don't have a team. Can I still play?

Yes. Choose "I need a team" in the form and we will do our best to connect solo players.

Can my business sponsor the event?

We would like to hear from you. Add a note in the form with your company name and we will be in touch.
